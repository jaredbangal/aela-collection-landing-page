Our Approach

Slow-made goods, sourced with care

Every maker on Aela Collection is chosen by hand. Small studios and independent craftspeople working in wood, clay, glass, and fiber. We visit the workshops, ask about the wood's origin and the glaze's chemistry, and only list what we'd bring into our own homes.

Prices go straight to the people who make the work. No warehouses, no markup layers. Just the maker, the material, and you.
